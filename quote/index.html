Request a quote

One email is enough — but the more of the six points below you fill in, the fewer questions come back and the sooner a price is on its way.

What to send

  1. The order code — six or seven digits, from the product page. If you have no code, send a photo of the part on the line.
  2. Size — DN or inch. Say which end the size refers to when the two differ.
  3. Material — aluminium, brass, 316, PP. If it is for a legal requirement, say so: we can supply material certificates.
  4. Quantity — pieces, and whether it is a one-off or a repeating order.
  5. Delivery — destination city and the date you need it by.
  6. Your details — company, country, and the person we should answer to.
