Couplings with a flange end

When the fixed side of the line is a flange rather than a thread, the coupling has to bolt on. This page collects every item in the range that ends in a flange, across all systems.

Which flange standard

Three families turn up most often in transfer work: DIN EN 1092-1 (PN 10 to PN 40), ANSI B16.5 (150 to 900 lb) and the truck flange to DIN 28459 / T.T.M.A. The bolt circle and gasket face decide what fits — a PN 16 flange and a 150 lb flange are close in size but not interchangeable.

Face and gasket

Flat face with a full-face gasket suits low pressure; raised face puts the load on a smaller area and needs a gasket sized to the raised ring. For aluminium bodies keep bolt torque modest — the flange will deform before the bolt yields.
