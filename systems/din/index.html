One programme, several standards

EN 14420 is not one coupling — it is a family of parts that share a hose end. Part 4 covers flanged hose tails, part 5 the screw-type LNC fittings, part 6 the TW tank-truck couplings, part 7 cam locking, part 8 Guillemin. They turn up on the same truck, which is why they are catalogued together.

The hose end is common

Whatever sits on the other end, the hose side follows one rule: a serrated or smooth tail with a collar, assembled with bolt-type safety clamps to EN 14420-3. A hose assembly can therefore change its coupling end without changing its hose end — and the clamp specification matters as much as the coupling.

ID codes are applications, not sizes

The German VG designations (VG 95950, VG 95952, VG 95954, VG 85281, VG 85289) say which vehicle or service a part was designed for — fuel and water replenishment, sea replenishment, Bundeswehr equipment. A part can meet EN 14420 and carry a VG code at the same time; the VG number narrows the application, not the dimensions.

Sizes and pressure

DN 20 to DN 200, PN 16 to PN 25 depending on the part. Pressure follows the body and the gasket, so check both when the duty is above PN 16.
