What makes a fitting sanitary

A sanitary coupling is not simply a polished one. It has to present no crevice where product can sit and no roughness that a cleaning cycle cannot reach. We machine to an inner surface of Ra 0.8 µm and an outer surface of Ra 1.6 µm, in low-carbon 316L (EN 1.4404) for corrosion resistance after welding.

Certification that customers ask for

American 3-A and European EHEDG are the two sets of rules buyers check. Our processes are certified to 3-A #3036 and we apply the 3-A symbol and EHEDG marks to the fittings they cover. Laser marking (SME, SS316L, 3-A, family, serial) is standard and can be changed on request.

From machining to packing

Parts leave the lathes into ultrasonic cleaning, which takes off the cutting oil, then are laser-marked and shrunk in foam so they stay dry and intact until they reach the line. Surface roughness is checked at the end of fabrication with a roughness gauge, and type tests are run whenever a new geometry is proven.

Choosing the family

Tri-clover clamps are the quickest to open; DIN 11851 is the European threaded standard; SMS and RJT are the other two you will meet in dairy and brewery work. The gasket is a separate item and belongs to the family, not to the coupling — state the compound when you order.
