What to include in your enquiry

The more of these you can give, the fewer round trips it takes:

  • Product family or part number (e.g. camlock Type C)
  • Size and thread standard — BSP, NPT or metric
  • Material: aluminium, stainless steel, brass or PP
  • Media and operating temperature
  • Seal preference: NBR, EPDM, FKM or PTFE
  • Quantity, and whether loose parts or assemblies

A photo or drawing is always welcome — send it to the same address.
