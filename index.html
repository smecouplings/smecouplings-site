Selection notes

Three questions usually settle the specification:

  • Media: what flows through it, and at what concentration?
  • Thread: BSP, NPT or metric — and parallel or tapered?
  • Service: static transfer, or repeated connect/disconnect under load?

Manufacturing

Casting, machining and finishing under one roof

Coupling bodies are cast or forged, then machined on CNC centres so that threads and cam grooves stay concentric. Surface treatment — anodising, zinc plating or passivation — is chosen per material and per end use.

Parts are checked dimensionally in batches before packing, and assemblies are supplied either loose or crimped with the hose and ferrule of your choice.
