About

A coupling is a small part with a large consequence

It is the joint that decides whether a transfer goes smoothly or spills. That is the standard we build to.

What we do

SME Couplings supplies hose couplings and fittings for industrial transfer: camlock, Storz, Guillemin, Bauer, EN 14420 tails, dry-break and bottom-loading couplers, sanitary unions, flanges and the associated clamps, seals and spare parts.

Parts are produced by casting or forging, machined on CNC centres, finished according to the material — anodising for aluminium, passivation for stainless, plating for steel and brass — then assembled and packed for export.

Because the same coupling body can serve very different media, much of the work is specification: matching material, seal compound and thread to the duty before anything is quoted.

We work with distributors, hose assemblers, tanker builders and OEMs, supplying either loose parts or complete crimped assemblies.

Capability

From drawing to packed carton

01 — Forming

Casting & forging

Gravity casting and hot forging of coupling bodies, cam arms, caps and plugs.

02 — Machining

CNC turning & milling

Threads, cam grooves and sealing faces cut to keep concentricity and repeatability.

03 — Finishing

Surface treatment

Anodising, zinc plating, passivation and polishing, selected per material.

04 — Assembly

Hose assemblies

Tails, ferrules and clamps crimped to length, tested and labelled.

05 — Inspection

Batch checking

Dimensional checks on threads, grooves and seals before packing.

06 — Packing

Export preparation

Cartons, pallets, moisture protection and shipping marks.
