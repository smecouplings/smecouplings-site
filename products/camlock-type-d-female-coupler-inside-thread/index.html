Camlock Type D female coupler, inside thread

Coupling end: Camlock female (coupler) Other end: BSPP female thread Materials: 316, Aluminium, Brass, Plastic Sizes: —–—

Type D is a female coupler with a female thread on the back. The thread takes a male pipe thread, and the coupler seals onto a camlock male adapter.

It is the mirror of Type A: same thread, but on the coupler body instead of the plug, so the quick connection sits on the pipe side rather than the hose side.

What to check before ordering

  • Parallel BSPP thread seals on the face; use the gasket, not thread tape.
  • Check that the mating thread is not BSPT (tapered) — the two are not interchangeable.
