Camlock Type DA coupling adapter, reducing

Coupling end: Camlock female (coupler) Other end: Camlock male (arm), smaller size Materials: Aluminium Sizes: —–—

Type DA joins a larger female coupler to a smaller male adapter in one part: a reducing coupling adapter with a coupler body at one end and an arm end at the other.

It removes the need for a separate reducer when a big line feeds a smaller hose.

What to check before ordering

  • Size is quoted as coupler size × plug size, for example 2" × 1-1/2".
  • Reducing adapters are not intended as a permanent step-down in a fixed pipeline.
