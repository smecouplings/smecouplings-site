Cam & Groove · AA59326 / EN 14420-7

Camlock Type E male adapter, hose shank

Coupling end: Camlock male (arm) Other end: Hose shank Materials: 316, Aluminium, Brass, Plastic Sizes: —–—

Type E is the hose-end plug: the shank goes into the hose and is clamped, and the arm end plugs into a camlock female coupler.

Together with Type C it forms the classic hose-to-hose or hose-to-pipe pair used on transfer lines.

What to check before ordering

  • The shank is serrated to grip the hose; do not smooth it or the clamp will slip.
  • Use a shank size that matches the hose bore, not the pipe size.
