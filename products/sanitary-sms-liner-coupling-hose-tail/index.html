SMS liner coupling, hose tail in 316L. Sanitary fittings are built to be cleaned: crevice-free, machined to a controlled roughness and assembled without a pocket where product can sit.

Sizes are DN; the tube outside diameter is given in the table.

What to check before ordering

  • Inner surface Ra 0.8 µm, outer Ra 1.6 µm as standard.
  • Gaskets are a separate item — state the compound for your cleaning regime.
  • 3-A and EHEDG certificates can be supplied with the goods.
