Guillemin · NF E29-572 / EN 14420-8

Guillemin blind end, locking plug

Coupling end: Blind cap Other end: Blind Materials: 316 Aluminium Brass PP Sizes: 1/2–6

Guillemin blind end, locking plug in 316, Aluminium, Brass, PP. The symmetric (Guillemin) coupling closes by drawing two hooks over the opposite rim and locking them; it is the everyday coupling on tank trucks and bulk transfer lines in France and much of Europe.

Sizes follow DN; the inch figure is nominal and given for reference.

What to check before ordering

  • Both halves must be the same Guillemin size and pressure class.
  • The gasket sits in the female rim — check it before every fill.
  • Hooks and springs are replaceable; a slack hook will not hold pressure.
