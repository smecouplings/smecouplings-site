Camlock Type B female coupler, outside thread

Coupling end: Camlock female (coupler) Other end: BSPP male thread Materials: 316 Aluminium Brass Plastic Sizes: 1/2–3/4

Type B is a female coupler with an outside (male) thread on the back. The coupler seals onto a camlock male adapter, while the thread screws into a female port.

Used where the fixed side of the system is a female threaded socket: a tank outlet, a valve body, a pump casing.

What to check before ordering

  • A gasket in the coupler is what seals the joint; check it before every connection.
  • Hand-tighten only — a camlock is not designed to be force-tightened with a wrench.
