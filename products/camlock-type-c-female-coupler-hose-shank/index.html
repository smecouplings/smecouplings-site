Camlock Type C female coupler, hose shank

Coupling end: Camlock female (coupler) Other end: Hose shank Materials: 316, Aluminium, Brass, Plastic Sizes: —–—

Type C joins a hose to a camlock male adapter. The serrated shank pushes into the hose and is secured with an interlocking clamp or a crimped ferrule; the coupler body then seals onto the male adapter.

This is the standard hose-end coupler for suction and discharge lines in water, chemical and tanker service.

What to check before ordering

  • Match the shank size to the hose bore, not to the thread size.
  • For permanent assemblies use a crimped ferrule; for field repair an interlocking clamp is enough.
