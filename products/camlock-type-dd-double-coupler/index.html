Camlock Type DD double coupler

Coupling end: Camlock female (coupler) Other end: Camlock female (coupler) Materials: Aluminium Sizes: —–—

Type DD is two female couplers back to back, joining two male adapters — for example two hose ends that both carry arm-type plugs.

Common as a hose-to-hose joiner and as an extension piece on transfer lines.

What to check before ordering

  • Both ends need their own gasket.
  • Not a substitute for a proper reducing adapter when the two ends differ in size.
