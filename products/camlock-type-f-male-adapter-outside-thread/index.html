Camlock Type F male adapter, outside thread

Coupling end: Camlock male (arm) Other end: BSPP male thread Materials: 316, Aluminium, Brass Sizes: —–—

Type F carries a male thread on the back and a camlock arm end at the front. The thread screws into a female port and the plug then accepts a camlock female coupler.

It puts the quick connection on equipment that is already threaded female — most common on pumps, filters and small tanks.

What to check before ordering

  • Male BSPP thread seals against a gasket in the female port, or with a bonded seal.
  • Do not use a tapered male thread in a parallel female port.
