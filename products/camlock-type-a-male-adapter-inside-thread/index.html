Camlock Type A male adapter, inside thread

Coupling end: Camlock male (arm) Other end: BSPP female thread Materials: 316, Aluminium, Brass, Plastic Sizes: 1/2"–8"

Type A is the adapter that puts a camlock connection onto a threaded pipe end. The arm end plugs into any camlock female coupler; the other end carries a female BSPP thread, so a male pipe thread screws straight into it.

It is the part you reach for when the line already ends in a thread — a pump port, a manifold outlet, a tank socket — and you want quick connect/disconnect downstream of it.

What to check before ordering

  • BSPP is a parallel thread: the seal is made on the gasket face, not on the threads.
  • Wrap PTFE tape on the pipe thread only to stop loosening, never to stop a leak.
  • The cam arm and the gasket are the wear parts; both are replaceable.
